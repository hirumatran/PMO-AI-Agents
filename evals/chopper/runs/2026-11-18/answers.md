### Q15
No clause in ISO 13485:2016 requires OEE, let alone 85%. The standard sets no production efficiency targets. 85% is a common TPM / Lean benchmark. Clauses 8.4 and 8.5.1 only require monitoring and improving QMS effectiveness. Do not cite ISO 13485 as the basis for an OEE target.
Source: ISO 13485:2016 (no OEE requirement)
